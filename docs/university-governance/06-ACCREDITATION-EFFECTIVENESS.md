# Accreditation Readiness and Institutional Effectiveness Plan

## Self-study status

This is a readiness framework, not an accreditation self-study submitted to or accepted by an accreditor. The institution has not supplied evidence of eligibility, candidacy, accreditation, degree authority, Title IV participation, or specialized program accreditation. All public claims remain blocked until decision letters are verified.

## Evidence domains

1. Mission, integrity and public information
2. Governance, autonomy and conflicts
3. Legal authority and institutional identity
4. Academic programs, rigor and credit
5. Faculty sufficiency and qualifications
6. Student admission, support, complaints and achievement
7. Library, learning and technology resources
8. Financial capacity, audit, reserves and teach-out
9. Institutional effectiveness and continuous improvement
10. Distance education, identity, privacy and accessibility
11. Responsible AI and human academic accountability
12. Records, disclosures, consumer protection and regulatory compliance

Each standard has a narrative, requirement crosswalk, evidence owner, verified exhibit, finding, gap, corrective action, deadline, validation method, and approval.

## Effectiveness cycle

Mission → strategic goals → program outcomes → course outcomes → curriculum and service delivery → direct/indirect evidence → analysis → decision → funded improvement → implementation → reassessment → public/accountable reporting.

## Annual institutional report

The report includes authority/accreditation status; governance actions; enrollment and applicant funnel; student composition using privacy-safe reporting; retention, progression, completion and time-to-award; course success and learning outcomes; complaints and appeals; accessibility; faculty sufficiency; library use; workforce/transfer outcomes with methodology; financial statements and sustainability; audits; cybersecurity and continuity exercises; risks; strategic-plan progress; and improvement commitments.

No rate is published without a defined cohort, denominator, period, exclusions, source system, reconciliation, privacy review, and responsible owner. No employment, salary, transfer, licensure, or completion outcome is guaranteed.

## Assessment governance

Institutional Effectiveness maintains the assessment calendar and evidence repository. Programs report annually; general education and administrative units follow an approved cycle. The Academic Council reviews learning evidence, executive leadership assigns resources, and the Board reviews institutional outcomes and risk. AI may summarize validated evidence but cannot invent findings or close actions.

## Readiness decision

Launch is blocked until authority, governance, audited financial capacity, academic staffing, student protection, technology/security, library resources, truthful catalog, complaint/appeal, records custody, accessibility, and teach-out controls are verified. Accreditation pursuit requires a Board-approved accreditor strategy and written eligibility confirmation.
