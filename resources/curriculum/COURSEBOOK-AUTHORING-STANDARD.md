# Coursebook Authoring and Resource Standard

Each course release must include a versioned syllabus, 15 instructional-equivalent hours per semester credit hour, measurable outcomes, weekly modules, original or cleared readings, lecture transcript, accessible slides, guided notes, glossary, practice, authentic assessment, rubric, source list, AI-use rules, human appeal route, and faculty approval.

## Required metadata

Course code; title; catalog description; credits/contact hours; prerequisites; program mapping; author; human academic owner; AI persona; version; effective term; last source review; next review; accessibility review; copyright/license; retention class; assessment security class.

## Lesson package

1. Essential question and relevance.
2. Learning outcomes using observable verbs.
3. Required reading and primary authorities.
4. Original explanation with examples and counterexamples.
5. HBCU-centered community, entrepreneurship, leadership, or public-interest case.
6. Guided practice and answer rationale.
7. Independent application.
8. Formative check and recovery route.
9. Reflection and next-step preview.

## Quality gates

- Every legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory claim has a current primary source and as-of date.
- No copied institutional course prose, publisher content, test items, or answer keys.
- No fabricated approvals, accreditation, transfer, licensing, or employment claims.
- AI content receives qualified human review before publication.
- Images have provenance, permission, captions, and alt text.
- Math, tables, and charts have text alternatives.
- Assessment banks are separated from learner-facing packages.
- Course completion cannot automatically issue a consequential credential.
