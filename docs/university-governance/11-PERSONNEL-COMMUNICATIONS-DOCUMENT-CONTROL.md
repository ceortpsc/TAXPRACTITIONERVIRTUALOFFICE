# University Personnel, Communications, and Document Control Manual

**Status:** Draft. No listed appointment, credential, mailbox, seal, stamp, or communications channel is active solely because it appears in this manual.

Human positions require an approved job description, minimum qualifications, verified CV/credential profile, decision authority, conflicts disclosure, supervisor, appointment record, security training, and periodic evaluation. AI personas are software assistants, never employees, licensed professionals, voting faculty, trustees, or final decision-makers. Their directories state purpose, data boundary, allowed tools, escalation owner, and human-approval rule.

Role mailbox names are reserved in the application directory but are not operational until the email administrator provisions them, configures authentication and retention, assigns delegates, tests inbound and outbound delivery, and records verification. Notification channels remain disabled until provider, consent, opt-out, template, delivery, retry, suppression, incident, and audit controls pass.

Letterhead, seals, stamps, signature blocks, and watermarks are controlled assets. Draft assets cannot claim accreditation, government recognition, degree authority, licensure approval, or an unverified officer signature. Every generated document records template version, data snapshot, author, approver, output hash, issuance state, recipient, delivery event, replacement, void, and retention class.

The student communication library contains 76 controlled templates spanning inquiry, admissions, enrollment, registration, attendance, advising, progress, standing, grading, integrity, assessment, accommodations, social-service referrals, financial-aid readiness, student billing, transcripts, record corrections, graduation, and conferment. Each remains a draft until its owning office, counsel where necessary, accessibility reviewer, and authorized approver release the exact language.
