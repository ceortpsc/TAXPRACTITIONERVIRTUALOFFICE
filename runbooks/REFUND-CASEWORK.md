# Refund casework runbook

Open a case only with authorization and permissible purpose. Verify identity, tax period, return receipt, transcript source, and notices. Normalize events without changing the source. TC 570 and TC 810 are indicators requiring research; they are not self-explanatory conclusions.

For non-receipt, confirm that a refund posted, capture issue date and method, apply current IRM 21.4.2 criteria, and document Form 3911 or oral-statement handling. For hardship, document immediate impact and prior attempts, then evaluate current TAS criteria. Every contact records time, channel, representative/function, reference, promise date, evidence, and next follow-up.

Close only with authoritative evidence: account resolution, trace disposition, replacement outcome, client withdrawal, or documented transfer. Never close merely because time elapsed.
