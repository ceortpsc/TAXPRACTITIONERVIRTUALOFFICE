# Virtual office employee handbook

Employees must use assigned accounts, MFA, approved devices, least privilege, clean-desk practices, and approved communication channels. Taxpayer information may be accessed only for assigned duties and never copied into personal storage or unapproved AI systems.

Required onboarding: identity verification, confidentiality agreement, security training, role assignment, supervised sandbox work, competency review, and manager activation. Offboarding immediately suspends sessions, revokes tools and keys, transfers cases, preserves required records, and reviews recent access.

Incidents, suspected identity theft, misdirected documents, suspicious requests, and unauthorized access are reported immediately. No retaliation applies to good-faith reporting.
