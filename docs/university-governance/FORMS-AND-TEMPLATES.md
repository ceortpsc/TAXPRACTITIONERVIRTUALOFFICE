# Governance and Academic Forms

## Board resolution

Resolution number; meeting date; authority; recitals; resolved actions; conditions; responsible officer; funding; effective date; review/expiration; vote; recusals; chair signature; secretary attestation; exhibits.

## Presidential directive

Directive number; title; purpose; authority; scope; definitions; policy/action; responsibilities; controls; reporting; effective date; expiration/review; legal/fiscal/technology impact; President signature.

## Curriculum minutes

Committee; meeting ID; date/time; participants; quorum; conflicts; proposal ID; evidence; discussion summary; motion; vote; conditions; effective term; owner; deadlines; approval signature.

## Course proposal

Identity; description; credits/time; prerequisites; rationale; outcomes; program map; weekly outline; resources; assessments; faculty; accessibility; modality; technology/AI/privacy; cost; approvals.

## Program proposal

Award; authority; need; admissions; completion; sequence; outcomes; curriculum map; faculty; library; services; assessment; budget; enrollment; consumer disclosures; authorization/accreditation; teach-out; approvals.

## Faculty credential review

Faculty ID; appointment; courses; degrees/transcripts; licenses; experience; teaching evidence; equivalency rationale; verifier; conflicts; approval; limits; review date.

## Student record correction

Request ID; student; record; current value; requested value; basis; evidence; privacy classification; faculty/department decision; Registrar verification; decision; appeal; immutable audit event.

## Graduation audit and conferment

Student/program/catalog; credits; GPA; courses; residency; practicum; capstone; standing; holds; authority; records verification; human reviewers; award date; credential ID; Registrar certification.
