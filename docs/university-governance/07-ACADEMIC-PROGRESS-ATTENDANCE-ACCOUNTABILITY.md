# Academic Progress, Attendance, GPA, and Accountability Policy

**Control status:** Draft for faculty, Registrar, accessibility, legal, and Board review. Not effective until adopted.

## Academic record and real-time presentation
The SIS maintains term, section, enrollment, assignment, submission, grade, attendance, degree-audit, intervention, appeal, and decision records. Every material change carries actor, timestamp, source, reason, version, tenant, and correlation data. “Real time” means event-driven display of the latest committed record; unverified activity is not an official grade or attendance event.

## GPA and grade controls
Unless an adopted catalog rule provides otherwise, A=4, B=3, C=2, D=1, and F=0 quality points. GPA is quality points divided by GPA credits and displayed to three decimals. Pass, no-pass, incomplete, and withdrawal records do not contribute quality points. Only authorized instructors or academic reviewers may approve grades. AI may provide formative feedback but cannot assign a final grade, alter the gradebook, decide an integrity allegation, or decide an appeal.

## Attendance, schedules, and expectations
Attendance evidence may be a verified live-session check-in, faculty verification, or meaningful academic activity; login alone is insufficient. The official schedule identifies timezone, modality, meetings, assessment windows, and deadlines. Degree audits distinguish earned, in-progress, planned, repeated, transferred, excluded, and required credits. Expected completion is an estimate, never a promise.

## Accountability and due process
Versioned rules may flag low averages, attendance concerns, missed work, inactivity, GPA thresholds, pace, or integrity concerns. A flag creates a human support/review task. It cannot automatically withdraw, fail, suspend, penalize, or withhold a credential. Before adverse action, the student receives notice, evidence access, response opportunity, accommodation review where applicable, a reasoned human decision, and an appeal route.

Draft thresholds are below 70% assignment average, below 80% verified attendance, and below 2.000 cumulative GPA. These are not enforceable until adopted and published for the applicable program and catalog year.

## Privacy and operational state
Students may view their records; faculty access is limited to assigned sections; Registrar and staff access follows job duty. Exports are logged and tenants isolated server-side. Code and schema may be configured before the SIS, queue, and notification infrastructure are operational. No live claim is permitted without end-to-end verification.
