# Charter, Bylaws and Governance Manual

## Charter and articles framework

The institution is proposed as a separately governed educational operation of Ross Tax Pro Software Co. Its purposes are online education in business, accounting, taxation, technology, leadership, adult education, and related fields; scholarly and applied inquiry; community economic advancement; and lawful certificate, diploma, and degree activity only within documented authority.

The final articles must identify the legal entity, registered agent, principal office, lawful nonprofit or for-profit character, governing law, member/shareholder relationship if any, board powers, educational purpose, limitations, conflicts, indemnification, dissolution, records custody, and amendment method. Corporate counsel must reconcile the articles with the parent entity and every state in which students are enrolled.

## Board of Trustees bylaws

The Board holds fiduciary responsibility for mission, integrity, executive oversight, financial sustainability, risk, academic quality, truthful public representation, and preservation of institutional records. It does not direct individual grading or admissions decisions except through approved policy and appeal authority.

### Composition and independence

The Board should include finance/accounting, higher education, legal/compliance, technology/cybersecurity, workforce, student-success, and community expertise. A majority-independent standard is recommended. Terms, removal, vacancies, compensation, annual disclosure, background review, and succession are recorded.

### Committees

- Executive and Governance
- Academic Quality and Student Success
- Audit, Risk, Finance and Compliance
- Technology, Data and Cybersecurity
- Nominations, Compensation and Executive Evaluation

### Meetings

Regular meetings occur at least quarterly. Notice, agenda, quorum, recusal, executive session, voting, minutes, written consent, remote participation, emergency action, and record retention follow adopted bylaws. Minutes record deliberative outcomes without exposing protected student, employee, legal, or security information.

## Shared governance

The Board reserves charter, presidential appointment, mission, strategy, budget, risk appetite, degree authority, major program approval, tuition, and closure decisions. The President administers the institution. The Provost and Academic Council govern curriculum and academic standards subject to Board authority. Faculty own course-level academic judgment. The Registrar owns official academic records. Compliance officers retain independent escalation access to the Audit and Risk Committee.

## Presidential directives

Directives use a unique number, authority citation, scope, findings, operative clauses, accountable executive, effective date, expiration/review date, fiscal and technology impacts, consultation record, and signature. Emergency directives expire within 90 days unless ratified.

## Mission and vision

**Mission:** Deliver rigorous, accessible, technology-enabled education in business, accounting, taxation, and leadership that advances ethical practice, economic mobility, community wealth, and public trust.

**Vision:** Become a distinguished online institution known for HBCU-centered excellence, applied scholarship, responsible AI, student support, and graduates prepared to lead with competence and conscience.

**Values:** truth; intellectual rigor; access; cultural dignity; stewardship; service; privacy; accountability; due process; innovation; and human responsibility.

## Five-year strategic plan, 2027-2031

1. **Authority and integrity:** secure applicable state authority; establish truthful disclosures; prepare accreditation eligibility without premature claims.
2. **Academic excellence:** approve coherent programs; credential faculty; build original/OER coursebooks; assess learning and close improvement loops.
3. **Student access and completion:** deploy accessible advising, early alerts, tutoring, complaint resolution, and measurable persistence interventions.
4. **Digital trust:** operate secure LMS/SIS/identity services, privacy governance, incident response, resilience, and responsible AI oversight.
5. **Sustainable growth:** maintain audited budgets, enrollment scenarios, reserves, teach-out capacity, workforce partnerships, and transparent outcomes.

Annual key results include approval milestones, course-review completion, learning-outcome attainment, retention, complaint resolution, accessibility remediation, faculty evaluation, security exercises, audit findings, and financial-reserve thresholds. Targets require Board adoption after baseline data exists.
