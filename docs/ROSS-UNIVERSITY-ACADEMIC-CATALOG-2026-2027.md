# Ross Tax Pro University
## Proposed Academic Catalog, Curriculum and Coursebook Resource Plan 2026-2027

**Owner:** Ross Tax Pro Software Co  
**Delivery:** 100% online, AI-assisted, human-accountable  
**Status:** Curriculum design and readiness artifact - not evidence of accreditation, state authorization, enrollment availability, transferability, or degree-awarding authority.

## 1. Institutional purpose

Ross Tax Pro University is designed as an access-centered online learning environment grounded in the historic HBCU commitments to intellectual excellence, economic mobility, civic leadership, cultural competence, and community advancement. Its academic method combines rigorous reading, quantitative reasoning, case analysis, writing, practical laboratories, oral defense, and reflective leadership.

The curriculum is original. Texas Southern University materials are used only as public benchmarks for program architecture. No affiliation, endorsement, equivalency, transfer guarantee, or authorization is claimed. Proposed degrees must remain unavailable until every applicable regulator and accreditor confirms authority in writing.

## 2. Academic quality framework

Every credit-bearing course is designed around 15 instructional-equivalent hours per semester credit hour, plus independent preparation. Each course package must contain:

1. Faculty-approved syllabus and measurable outcomes.
2. Original coursebook or lawfully licensed/open educational resources.
3. Weekly lesson, lecture transcript, accessible slides, guided notes, and glossary.
4. Primary-source reading packet and source annotations.
5. Worked examples, practice set, case or laboratory, and discussion protocol.
6. Low-stakes knowledge checks with feedback.
7. Authentic assessment, scoring rubric, and accessibility alternative.
8. Citation guide, academic-integrity notice, AI-use disclosure, and human appeal route.
9. Version, owner, last review date, next review date, and change log.

AI faculty may explain, demonstrate, tutor, generate practice, and score objective items. Human academic officers approve curricula, subjective grades, appeals, accommodations, integrity decisions, completion, transcripts, and credentials.

## 4. Proposed associate pathways

### Associate of Arts in Business Administration - 60 SCH

The proposed plan combines a general education foundation with the Texas Business Administration Field of Study: financial and managerial accounting, macroeconomics, microeconomics, business principles, business mathematics, statistics, and business applications. A sample four-term sequence is:

- **Term 1:** Composition I; Business Principles; Business Mathematics; Professional Communication; first-year seminar.
- **Term 2:** Composition II; Macroeconomics; Financial Accounting; United States History I; Creative Arts.
- **Term 3:** Microeconomics; Managerial Accounting; Business Applications; United States History II; Life and Physical Science I.
- **Term 4:** Business Statistics; Federal Government; Texas Government; Life and Physical Science II; Language, Philosophy and Culture; elective.

### Associate of Arts in Accounting - 60 SCH

Adds Accounting Information Systems and Payroll Accounting to the business foundation. Students complete a closing-cycle simulation, payroll controls case, spreadsheet model, and ethical-risk memo using synthetic data.

These are proposed degrees. The Texas field-of-study mapping does not itself make courses transferable or authorize the institution to award degrees.

## 6. Adult education and equivalency

### Texas GED Fast-Track

The preparatory sequence covers the four current GED subjects: Reasoning Through Language Arts, Mathematical Reasoning, Science, and Social Studies. The program uses diagnostic placement, individualized study plans, skill lessons, timed practice, readiness conferences, and official-testing referral.

Ross Tax Pro University does not issue the Texas Certificate of High School Equivalency. Texas issues the official credential after qualifying examination. Legacy HiSET and TASC records may appear in state verification systems, but incomplete HiSET testing cannot presently be finished in Texas. Marketing must not call preparation a diploma program.

### Adult foundational education

- Writing, grammar, and argument
- Numeracy through algebraic reasoning
- Scientific inquiry and data interpretation
- Civic literacy and United States history

## 7. Texas home-education high-school pathway

Texas recognizes bona fide home schools using a written curriculum that includes reading, spelling, grammar, mathematics, and good citizenship. The parent or legal guardian remains the school administrator and diploma authority. The Ross pathway supplies curriculum, records, rubrics, and advising; it must not imply that TEA accredits, approves, or issues a homeschool diploma.

The 22-credit pathway voluntarily uses the Texas Foundation High School Program as a rigor benchmark:

- English language arts: 4 credits
- Mathematics: 3 credits
- Science: 3 credits
- Social studies: 3 credits
- Languages other than English: 2 credits
- Physical education: 1 credit
- Fine arts: 1 credit
- Electives: 5 credits

Good citizenship is integrated through Federal Government, Texas Government, civic action, media literacy, constitutional principles, community responsibility, and respectful public deliberation. Parents retain attendance, portfolio, transcript, course-completion, and diploma records.

## 8. Coursebook architecture

Each coursebook is authored as eight parts:

1. **Orientation:** essential question, relevance, diagnostic, vocabulary.
2. **Concepts:** concise chapters with sources and counterexamples.
3. **Methods:** worked processes, calculation protocols, research strategies.
4. **Cases:** HBCU-centered entrepreneurship, public service, community wealth, ethics, and technology contexts.
5. **Laboratory:** synthetic documents, spreadsheets, datasets, and simulations.
6. **Seminar:** discussion questions, structured debate, peer response, oral defense.
7. **Assessment:** practice, project, exam, rubric, mastery recovery.
8. **Reference:** glossary, formulas, authorities, bibliography, index, accessibility statement.

### Model course: ACCT 2301 Financial Accounting

**Outcomes:** explain the accounting equation; analyze transactions; prepare adjusting and closing entries; produce and interpret primary financial statements; evaluate internal controls; use spreadsheets; and communicate ethical implications.

**Units:** accounting as an information system; transaction analysis; accrual accounting; merchandising; cash and internal control; receivables; inventory; long-lived assets; liabilities; equity; cash flows; financial-statement analysis.

**Assessments:** weekly mastery checks 15%; laboratories 25%; case memoranda 20%; midterm 15%; capstone financial-statement project and oral defense 25%.

**Resources:** OpenStax *Principles of Financial Accounting* or another faculty-cleared OER; FASB conceptual materials where lawfully accessible; SEC EDGAR public-company filings; original Ross laboratory workbook; spreadsheet templates; accessibility-ready lecture transcripts.

### Model course: TAX 2304 IRS Procedure and Notices

**Outcomes:** classify common notices; identify deadlines; build a chronology; distinguish taxpayer, preparer, and representative authority; retrieve controlling public guidance; draft a review-ready response; and escalate matters outside scope.

**Units:** administration and authority; taxpayer accounts; notices; examination overview; collection overview; appeals; authorization; records and evidence; penalty framework; identity verification; case closure.

**Resources:** Internal Revenue Code and Treasury regulations; current IRS publications, forms, instructions, Internal Revenue Manual, Taxpayer Advocate Service materials, and Tax Court opinions. Every lesson requires an as-of date and source check because procedural material changes.

### Model course: GED-MATH Mathematical Reasoning

**Outcomes:** reason with rational numbers; ratios, percentages, and units; solve equations and inequalities; interpret functions; apply geometry; analyze tables and graphs; and explain a solution path.

**Units:** quantitative fluency; proportional reasoning; algebraic expressions; linear equations; functions; geometry; statistics and probability; multi-step test strategy.

## 9. Assessment and academic integrity

Objective items may be machine-scored. Essays, projects, oral defenses, appeals, integrity findings, accommodations, and final award decisions require an authorized human. AI-use rules distinguish prohibited substitution from permitted tutoring, brainstorming, feedback, citation support, and accessibility assistance. Students receive notice, evidence, an opportunity to respond, and an appeal route before adverse academic action.

## 10. Faculty and AI governance

AI Chancellor, Dean, Faculty Instructor, Tutor, Registrar Assistant, Student Success Coach, Instructional Designer, and Assessment Assistant operate from approved knowledge sources, versioned prompts, least privilege, usage limits, redaction, audit correlation, and timeout controls. AI personas do not hold employment, licensure, academic rank, or legal authority. Human officers remain accountable.
