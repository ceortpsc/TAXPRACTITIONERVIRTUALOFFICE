# Degree, Certificate and Mandated-Program Document Standard

Every program receives one controlled master file containing the following sections.

## Program overview

Official title, award, school/department, delivery mode, CIP/SOC if applicable, purpose, audience, authority status, accreditation status, truthful disclosure, program length, credits/hours, costs, occupational claims, and effective catalog year.

## Admission requirements

Published academic prerequisites, age/education evidence, identity process, language/technology readiness, transfer/advanced-standing rules, essential requirements, nondiscrimination, accommodations, deadlines, fees, decision authority, reconsideration, and privacy notice. Criminal-history or licensure barriers may be stated only after legal review.

## Completion requirements

Minimum credits/hours, required courses, grades/GPA, residency, practicum, capstone, competency assessment, time limit, financial/records clearance, conduct standards, application for completion, and human conferment approval.

## Pathway map and sequence

Term-by-term sequence identifies prerequisites, co-requisites, milestones, assessments, work-integrated learning, electives, stop-out/reentry points, and stackable awards. Published plans include a disclaimer that course availability and external transfer are not guaranteed.

## Practicum or internship

Site agreement, qualified supervisor, learning contract, hours/activity log, safety, confidentiality, accessibility, insurance where applicable, background checks where lawful, evaluation, incident reporting, complaint route, and alternative placement. Tax practicums use synthetic data until client-data authority is separately approved.

## Capstone

Students integrate program outcomes through an authentic project, source-based analysis, portfolio, presentation, and oral defense. Faculty approve topic, ethics/privacy plan, rubric, authorship evidence, revision, and final result.

## Assessment plan

For every outcome: direct measure, benchmark, sample, collection point, responsible faculty, disaggregation rules, analysis date, finding, action, owner, completion date, and reassessment. Small-cell suppression protects privacy. Results improve the program and are not manufactured for accreditation optics.

## Court-mandated or externally required programs

No program is marketed as court-approved, probation-approved, state-approved, or satisfying an order without written recognition from the controlling authority. Attendance verification uses authenticated activity plus substantive work; completion certificates include program version, verified hours, identity method, issuer, and limitations. Staff never promise acceptance by a court or agency.

## Program handbook contents

Welcome; contacts; outcomes; plan; academic expectations; technology; attendance/engagement; grading; integrity/AI; practicum/capstone; advising; accessibility; nondiscrimination; privacy; complaints; appeals; conduct; costs/refunds; records/transcripts; completion; external credential disclosures; safety; teach-out; and acknowledgment.

## Specialized accreditation

The file identifies whether specialized accreditation is required, optional, unavailable, or not applicable. Evidence includes eligibility correspondence, standards crosswalk, self-study, exhibits, site/virtual review, decision letter, conditions, monitoring, public disclosure, and renewal. “Accredited” appears only after a recognized accreditor issues a current decision covering the institution/program.
