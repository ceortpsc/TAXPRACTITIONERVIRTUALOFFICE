# Online Bookstore, EPUB, and Course Resource Policy

**Control status:** Draft for academic, accessibility, copyright, finance, tax, privacy, and Board review.

Course materials must be mapped to an approved course and term. Each resource records format, edition, requirement level, accessibility validation, copyright or open-license basis, price, provider, availability, and review date. Students must see multiple lawful options when available: institution-included materials, open educational resources, publisher-direct access, authorized rental, and authorized print-on-demand.

Institution-authored EPUB releases must use EPUB 3, semantic structure, navigation, alternative text, reading order, language metadata, accessible tables and mathematics, and validation before publication. Source, build artifact, rights record, accessibility report, version, and hash remain controlled records.

The storefront separates catalog discovery, entitlement, order, payment, fulfillment, refund, and support states. No resource is represented as licensed or available until rights and fulfillment are verified. No paid checkout is enabled until merchant-of-record, sales-tax, pricing, refund, payment-security, reconciliation, accessibility, privacy, and customer-support controls pass production review. AI may recommend mapped resources but cannot purchase, change prices, grant paid entitlements, or assert copyright permission.
