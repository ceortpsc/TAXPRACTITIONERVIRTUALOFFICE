# Student Services Department Operations Manual

**Status:** Controlled draft pending executive, academic, privacy, accessibility, legal, and Board adoption.

The unified Student Services Division contains Admissions; Enrollment Services; Registrar; Academic Advising; Counseling and Student Success; Social Services; Financial Aid Readiness; Bursar and Student Billing; Institutional Accounting; and University Administration. Each department has documented authority, position descriptions, minimum qualifications, supervision, service targets, escalation paths, confidentiality controls, records ownership, and separation of duties.

## Intake and communications

Every contact receives a correlation identifier, requester type, verified contact channel, department, topic, subject, message, urgency, consent version, accessibility request, source, timestamps, assigned employee, status, service target, internal notes, approved responses, attachments, delivery events, and closure reason. Public forms never request SSNs, tax records, payment credentials, diagnosis details, identity documents, or authentication secrets. Sensitive communications move to the authenticated portal.

Outbound material statements, aid eligibility decisions, account credits/refunds, record changes, policy exceptions, liability admissions, crisis communications, and public statements require authorized human approval. AI may classify, summarize, recommend knowledge, translate, and draft; it may not decide admission, aid, academic standing, billing relief, disability accommodation, crisis disposition, discipline, or credential status.

## Counseling and social services

Student-success counseling is educational support unless licensed clinical services are separately established. Emergency statements receive clear emergency-service routing. Social-service referrals require informed consent, minimum-necessary data, verified providers, follow-up, and documented closure. The institution does not guarantee third-party eligibility or availability.

## Financial aid and billing

Financial Aid Readiness provides information and document-planning only until institutional Title IV eligibility and approved systems are verified. No FAFSA transaction, ISIR, packaging, award, disbursement, return-of-funds calculation, or NSLDS reporting is enabled by this draft. Billing ledgers use immutable entries, dual approval for credits/refunds/write-offs, reconciliation, aging, close controls, and audit evidence. Live payment processing remains disabled.
